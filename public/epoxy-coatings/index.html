    Why Epoxy

    The Smart Choice for Arizona Floors

    Epoxy coatings turn boring concrete into a floor that looks amazing and holds up to anything. No more oil stains. No more cracks hiding under a coat of paint. Just a clean, tough surface that you are proud to show off.

    In Arizona, regular concrete gets beat up fast. The heat causes it to crack. Oil and chemicals soak right in. And it looks dirty no matter how much you clean it. Epoxy fixes all of that.

    • Extremely tough — handles cars, forklifts, and heavy tools
    • Built for Arizona heat — will not peel or bubble
    • Slip-resistant texture keeps your family safe
    • Easy to clean — spills wipe right up
    • Looks like a showroom floor for years
    • 68+ color options — flake blends and solid metallics

    How It Works

    Our 6-Step Epoxy Process

    Prep is everything. We never skip steps — that is why our coatings last for years.

    1
    Free Inspection
    We look at your floor, check for cracks and moisture, and give you a written quote. No charge.
    2
    Diamond Grind
    We grind the concrete to open the pores. This is what makes the epoxy bond and last for years — not months.
    3
    Crack Repair
    We fill any cracks or divots before we coat. You get a smooth, even surface from edge to edge.
    4
    Base Coat
    High-build epoxy base coat goes down. This is the foundation that everything else bonds to.
    5
    Color Flakes or Metallic
    Decorative flakes or metallic pigment broadcast over the base. You pick the color — we make it look great.
    6
    Top Coat & Walk-Through
    Clear polyaspartic top coat goes on last. Then we walk the floor with you before we leave.
