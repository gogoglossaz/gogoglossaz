Why It Matters

Your Pool Deck Works Hard Every Day

Pool decks take a beating. Wet feet, sunscreen, chlorine splash, and Arizona sun all wear the surface down fast. Without a seal, pavers fade, stain, and get slippery and rough.

A good seal brings back the color. It locks out stains. It makes the surface safer when it is wet. And it keeps it looking great all year — not just when it is brand new.

We clean the deck first, fix any loose sand between pavers, and apply a sealer made for wet outdoor areas. We always add grip so you never have to choose between looks and safety.

  • Cool on bare feet — reflective sealers reduce heat
  • Anti-slip finish — safe for kids, adults, and pets
  • Stops sunscreen and leaf stains from soaking in
  • Brings back faded color in one visit
  • Keeps joint sand locked tight — fewer weeds and ants
  • Works on travertine, concrete pavers, brick, and more
