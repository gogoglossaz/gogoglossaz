    Why It Matters

    Floors That Work as Hard as You Do

    Bare concrete in your home looks unfinished and is a pain to keep clean. Dirt grinds into it. Spills stain it. It cracks over time from Arizona's heat and dry air.

    A residential epoxy coating fixes all of that. It seals the concrete so nothing soaks in. It looks great — you can pick from flake blends or rich metallic colors. And it is incredibly easy to clean. One mop and it is done.

    We work in every area of the home — patios, laundry rooms, utility areas, basements, and converted bonus rooms. Whatever you need covered, we can coat it.

    • Seals out stains, moisture, and mold
    • Safe for pets and kids — non-toxic once cured
    • Works indoors and on covered outdoor areas
    • Slip-resistant — safe even when wet
    • Available in 25 flake colors and 43 metallics
    • Lasts 10 to 20 years with normal care

    How It Works

    Our Residential Epoxy Process

    1
    Free Quote Visit
    We come out, look at your space, and bring color samples. Written quote before we leave. No charge.

    Prep & Grind
    We grind the concrete, fill cracks, and clean everything. Good prep is what makes the coating stick and last.
    3
    Base Coat & Color
    Epoxy base coat goes down, then your color flakes or metallic pigment. The transformation happens fast.
    4
    Top Coat & Done
    Clear polyaspartic top coat seals everything in. Walk-through with you. Walk on it in hours.
