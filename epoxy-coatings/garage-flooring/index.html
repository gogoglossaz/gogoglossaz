    Why It Matters

    Your Garage Floor Deserves Better

    Bare concrete in your garage looks bad and gets worse every year. Oil soaks in and never comes out. Tires leave marks that just bake into the surface. The heat causes cracks that spread over time.

    A garage floor epoxy coating changes everything. It seals the concrete completely so nothing soaks in. It looks sharp and clean. And it is so easy to sweep and mop — even motor oil wipes right up.

    We use a full diamond grind system — not the cheap roll-on kits from the hardware store. That is why our coatings bond deep and last for years without peeling or bubbling.

    • Oil, grease, and chemicals wipe right off
    • Tire marks do not stick or stain
    • Slip-resistant texture — safe even when wet
    • Built for Arizona heat — will not peel or bubble
    • 25+ flake colors and 43 metallic options
    • Lasts 10 to 20 years with normal use

    How It Works

    Our Garage Floor Coating Process

    1
    Free Quote Visit
    We measure your garage, check the concrete, and bring color samples. You get a written quote before we leave. No pressure.
    2
    Diamond Grind & Prep
    We grind the concrete so the epoxy bonds deep. Then we fill cracks and clean the whole floor. Prep is what makes it last.
    3
    Base Coat & Flakes
    High-build epoxy base coat goes down. Then we broadcast your color flakes or metallic pigment over the whole floor.
    4
    Top Coat & Walk-Through
    We seal everything with a clear polyaspartic top coat. Then we walk the floor with you. You can walk on it in hours.
