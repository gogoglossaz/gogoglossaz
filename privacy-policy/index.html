Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who We Are

Go Go Gloss LLC ("Go Go Gloss," "we," "us") provides epoxy floor coatings and paver sealing in Scottsdale and Metro Phoenix, Arizona. This policy explains what information we collect through gogoglossaz.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, schedule an estimate, call, or email us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your property address, city, and ZIP code
  • Your preferred date and time for an estimate
  • Details about your project and any notes you include

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It may collect your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, time on site, and the site that referred you. This information is used in aggregate and is not used to identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote request and contact you about your project
  • To schedule, perform, and follow up on our services
  • To send estimates, invoices, and service reminders
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed for them to do their job:

  • Formspree, which processes the quote and contact forms on our site
  • Google Analytics, which provides website usage statistics
  • Our website hosting provider

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or property, or in connection with a sale or transfer of our business.

Phone Calls, Texts, and Email

If you give us your phone number or email, we may call, text, or email you about your quote, appointment, or project. We will not send you marketing messages without your permission. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying STOP to a text, or by calling or emailing us.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on. The site will still work without cookies.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep customer and project records for as long as needed to provide service, honor any warranty, and meet tax and legal record-keeping requirements. Quote requests that don't turn into a job are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, and our site is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Links to Other Sites

Our site may link to other websites, such as Google reviews or social media. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
