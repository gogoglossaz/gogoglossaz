    How we collect, use, and protect your information

    Last updated: October 24, 2026

    Go Go Gloss ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website gogoglossaz.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or contact us for services.

    Information We Collect

    We may collect the following types of information:

    • Contact information — name, phone number, email address, and service address when you submit a quote request or contact form.

    How We Use Your Information

    • To respond to your quote requests and schedule service appointments

    We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics collects data such as pages visited, session duration, and general location.     We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law.

    • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
