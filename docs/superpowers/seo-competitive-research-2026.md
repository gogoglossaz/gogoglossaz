# SEO Competitive Research Report — Go Go Gloss
**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Analyst:** Go Go Gloss SEO Team
**Status:** Draft

---

## Executive Summary

Go Go Gloss enters a competitive but winnable market. For paver sealing, the top-ranking Phoenix/Scottsdale competitors (Arizona Paver Restoration, Phoenix Paver Sealing, Pro Seal AZ, and Paver Sealing Arizona) lead with experience claims, satisfaction guarantees, and climate-specific messaging, but none publish pricing on their pages — a transparency gap Go Go Gloss can exploit. For epoxy coatings, Viking Concrete Coatings dominates the Scottsdale SERP by being the only major competitor covering both paver sealing AND epoxy under one brand — the exact same dual-service positioning Go Go Gloss owns. Two Brothers Epoxy and EpoxyAZ.com have strong Phoenix presences with warranties as their primary differentiator.

The three highest-priority actions are: (1) Publish pricing transparency content — ballpark ranges, cost calculators, or cost guide blog posts — since no competitor does this on service pages, making it a significant trust and ranking signal opportunity; (2) Aggressively build out Google Business Profile with photos, Q&A, and service categories for both service lines, as local pack rankings drive the majority of service-area clicks; and (3) Create a dedicated "Travertine Pool Deck Sealing" landing page targeting Scottsdale specifically, as this is a high-value, high-volume keyword with thin coverage from most competitors.

---

## Methodology

Research was conducted on 2026-07-28 via live web search across four target queries: "paver sealing Scottsdale AZ," "paver sealing Phoenix AZ," "epoxy garage floor Phoenix AZ," and "epoxy coatings Scottsdale." The top 5–9 organic results per query were identified. Key competitor pages were fetched and analyzed for page title, H1, content structure, keyword targeting, pricing transparency, and unique selling proposition. Additional searches were run to benchmark pricing data and travertine-specific keyword competition. All competitor data reflects what is publicly visible as of the research date.

## Content Opportunities

### Underserved Topics in This Market

1. **Transparent Pricing Content** — Only PHXPaver publishes pricing ($1.50–$3.00/sq ft for sealing; $4–$8/sq ft for epoxy). Creating a dedicated "How Much Does Paver Sealing Cost in Phoenix?" blog post or cost guide page would capture high-intent research traffic that competitors send to Angi and HomeGuide.

2. **Sealer Type Comparisons** — No competitor has a strong "matte vs. gloss vs. wet-look sealer" comparison page. This is an active question pool homeowners search before hiring.

3. **Pool Deck Safety / Anti-Slip Content** — Only Viking mentions anti-slip as a feature. A dedicated "Pool Deck Paver Sealing: Anti-Slip Options for Arizona Pools" page would target a safety-concerned audience with limited competition.

4. **Before/After Case Study Posts** — Competitors show before/after galleries, but none publish written case studies (project type, square footage, products used, before state, process, result). These rank well for long-tail project searches.

5. **Arizona-Specific Seasonal Content** — "Best Time to Seal Pavers in Phoenix" and "How to Prep Your Paver Driveway Before Arizona Monsoon Season" are underserved topics that align with real homeowner search behavior.

6. **Polyaspartic vs. Epoxy Explainer** — Two Brothers Epoxy briefly touches on this but no one owns a comprehensive comparison piece. High conversion potential for floor coating shoppers.

7. **HOA-Compliant Coatings Guide for Scottsdale** — Many Scottsdale neighborhoods have strict HOA rules on surface appearance. A guide on HOA-compliant sealer finishes and coating colors for popular Scottsdale communities would be highly differentiated content.

8. **Commercial Epoxy Flooring for Phoenix Restaurants / Medical Offices** — Go Go Gloss has commercial pages but competitors like Two Brothers list 7 verticals. Expanding commercial sub-industry content would capture B2B searches.

## Google Business Profile Recommendations

Based on what competitors are doing (and not doing), Go Go Gloss should build GBP with:

1. **Two Separate Service Category Listings if possible** — or one listing with both "Paver Sealing" and "Epoxy Flooring" as primary/secondary categories. Use: Paving Contractor + Floor Refinishing Service or Floor Coating Company.

2. **Photos (minimum 30 at launch):** Before/after splits for paver sealing (pool deck, driveway, travertine), epoxy garage floors, and commercial projects. Include team/truck photos for local trust.

3. **Services Section:** List every service sub-category with descriptions — pool deck sealing, driveway sealing, travertine sealing, brick paver sealing, paver stripping, epoxy garage flooring, metallic epoxy, polyaspartic, commercial epoxy.

4. **Q&A Section:** Pre-populate with the FAQ questions listed above (cost questions, drying time, monsoon season, etc.). Competitors largely ignore this feature.

5. **Google Posts:** Publish at least 1 post per week — seasonal tips ("Seal before monsoon season"), project spotlights, limited-time offers. Viking and most competitors don't post regularly.
