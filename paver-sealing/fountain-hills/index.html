Paver Sealing in Fountain Hills

Beautiful Town, Beautiful Pavers — Keep Them That Way

Fountain Hills is one of the most scenic communities in the Valley. Homes here often have stunning desert views and beautiful outdoor living spaces with paver patios, pool decks, and driveways.

The elevation in Fountain Hills does not protect pavers from Arizona sun. UV rays are intense, and temperature swings between day and night can cause pavers to shift and crack over time. Sealing prevents that.

We make the drive out to Fountain Hills regularly. Our commercial-grade sealers protect pavers from UV fading, heat damage, and the monsoon rains that roll through each summer.
