Why It Matters

Your Driveway Takes a Beating Every Day

Cars, oil drips, Arizona heat, and monsoon rain hit your driveway every single day. Over time, the color fades, weeds push up between the pavers, and stains soak in deep.

A sealed driveway fights all of that. The seal locks the color in. It stops oil and stains from soaking into the stone. It keeps the joint sand tight so weeds have no way in.

We power wash your driveway first, replace any worn-out joint sand, and apply a commercial-grade sealer. You get a driveway that looks sharp and stays that way.

  • Stops oil and grease stains from soaking in
  • Brings back faded color in one visit
  • Locks joint sand — fewer weeds and ants
  • Holds up under daily car traffic
  • Protects against Arizona heat and UV rays
  • Wet-look or natural matte finish — your choice

How It Works

Our 4-Step Driveway Process

1
Free Inspection
We look at your driveway and tell you exactly what we will do. Free. No pressure. No surprise fees.
2
Power Wash
We blast off dirt, stains, and old sand. We also remove any weeds that pushed through the joints.
3
Re-Sand & Seal
We sweep in fresh joint sand and apply a commercial-grade sealer. Two coats for full protection.
4
Final Check
We walk the driveway with you before we leave. If anything is not right, we fix it. Simple as that.
