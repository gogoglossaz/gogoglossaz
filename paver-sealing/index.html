The Basics

What Is Paver Sealing?

Paver sealing puts a clear protective coat on your outdoor pavers. This coat locks out water, dirt, weeds, and stains. Your pavers stay cleaner and stronger for years.

Think of it like waxing a car. The wax protects the paint. A sealer protects your pavers. Without it, pavers fade, crack, and get stained over time.

In Arizona, sealing is even more important. Our intense sun, heat, and monsoon rain beat up unprotected pavers fast. A professional seal keeps them looking new.

  • Stops weeds from growing between pavers
  • Prevents stains from oil, food, and rust
  • Brings back the original color of your pavers
  • Makes pavers easier to clean and maintain
  • Protects against Arizona UV rays and heat
  • Helps sand stay in place between joints

Why It Matters Here

Arizona Is Tough on Pavers

Our desert climate is beautiful. But it is hard on outdoor surfaces. Here is what happens to pavers with and without sealing.

Without Sealing

Your Pavers Break Down Fast

  • Arizona sun bleaches and fades paver color
  • Heat causes pavers to crack and shift
  • Monsoon rain washes away joint sand
  • Weeds push up through the joints
  • Oil and rust stains are almost impossible to remove
  • Pavers become slippery and dangerous
  • Repair costs add up fast

With Go Go Gloss Sealing

Your Pavers Stay Beautiful

  • UV-resistant sealer locks in your paver color
  • Sealer flexes with the heat — no cracking
  • Polymeric sand stays locked in place
  • Weeds cannot push through the sealed joints
  • Stains wipe off easily before they set
  • Anti-slip additives keep surfaces safe
  • Pavers last much longer — save money long term
