When You Need Stripping

Signs Your Old Sealer Needs to Come Off

Old sealer does not always age gracefully. When it goes bad, you can see it — white patches, peeling sheets, or a cloudy haze that will not wash off no matter how hard you scrub.

Sealing over bad sealer makes it worse. The new coat can not bond to the old one. It peels faster and looks worse. The right move is to strip the old coat first, let the pavers breathe, and then apply a fresh seal.

We use safe chemical strippers made for each surface type. The stripper dissolves the old sealer without hurting the stone. Then we rinse, let it dry, and you are ready for a fresh coat.

  • Removes white or cloudy sealer that will not clean off
  • Takes off peeling or flaking old coats
  • Lets trapped moisture escape from under the surface
  • Prepares the paver for a fresh seal that bonds right

How It Works

Our 4-Step Stripping Process

1
Free Inspection
We look at the old sealer and tell you what we will do. We check the surface type and pick the right stripper.
2
Apply Stripper
We apply a safe chemical stripper that breaks down the old sealer without hurting the stone underneath.
3
Rinse & Dry
We power wash the stripper and old sealer off. We let the surface fully dry before anything else goes on.
4
Reseal (Optional)
We can apply a fresh seal right away or come back another day. Your pavers are clean and ready either way.
