Why It Matters

Arizona Sun Is Hard on Brick

Brick pavers start off rich and full of color. But Arizona's intense sun bleaches them fast. After a few years, the deep reds and tans fade to a pale, washed-out gray.

Sealing brings that color back. The right sealer soaks into the brick, wakes up the pigment, and adds a protective layer that keeps stains out and moisture from cracking the surface.

We clean the brick first, sweep in fresh joint sand, and seal with a product built for Arizona. You pick natural or wet-look. Either way, it looks rich — not fake.

  • Brings back deep reds and tans in one visit
  • Stops stains from soaking into the brick
  • Water beads off instead of cracking the surface
  • Locks joint sand — fewer weeds and ants
  • Natural or wet-look finish — your choice
  • Built for Arizona heat and UV rays
