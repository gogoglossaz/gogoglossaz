Why Phoenix Pavers Need Sealing

Phoenix Heat Is Hard on Pavers

Phoenix is one of the hottest cities in the United States. Pavement temperatures can hit 150°F in summer. That extreme heat causes pavers to crack, fade, and shift over time.

Add in monsoon rain that washes out joint sand, and UV rays that bleach color — unprotected pavers age fast in Phoenix. A professional seal changes that.

We have sealed driveways in Biltmore, patios in Arcadia, and pool decks in Ahwatukee. Phoenix homeowners trust us because we use the right products for desert conditions.

  • UV-block coating to stop color fading
