Why It Matters

Travertine Looks Great — Until It Doesn't

Travertine is a natural stone that has tiny holes and pores all over it. That is what gives it its beautiful texture. But those same holes let water, dirt, and pool chemicals soak right in.

In Arizona, unprotected travertine gets hit with intense UV rays, extreme heat, and pool chemicals all year. Without a seal, it absorbs stains, develops white mineral deposits, and starts to look dull and rough.

The right sealer soaks into the stone and fills those pores from the inside. It stops water and chemicals before they can cause damage. And it does all this without making the surface slippery or changing the natural color you love.

  • Protects from pool chemicals, chlorine, and hard water stains
  • Stops dirt and sunscreen from soaking into the pores
  • Keeps the natural travertine look — no shiny or wet finish
  • Anti-slip options keep pool decks safe when wet
  • Blocks Arizona UV rays from fading the stone
  • Easier to clean — dirt wipes right off the surface
